Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for new delivery customers.

What is the minimum order?

The minimum order is 15 loaves per store. You can order any whole-number quantity above 15 — for example 16, 23, 37 or 50.

How much is delivery?

The first delivery to a store during the day has a R5 delivery fee. A second delivery to the same store on the same day is free.

Where do you deliver?

Our current service area is Osizweni and Madadeni.

How do I place an order?

Use the order form above. It calculates your estimated total and prepares a WhatsApp order for confirmation.

Can I order more than 500 loaves?

Our stated daily supply capability is 500+ loaves. Larger requirements should be discussed with us in advance so we can confirm stock and route capacity.
